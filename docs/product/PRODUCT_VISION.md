# PRODUCT VISION — MENTE DE ACERO V2

## Mission & Purpose
Transform "Mente de Acero" into an empowering, clinical-grade psychological assessment and mental conditioning ecosystem for users aged 18–33. The platform combines institutional character strength with self-knowledge, confidentiality, well-being, and continuous personal growth.

## Core Pillars
1. **Psychometric Rigor:** EMA, Bar-On ICE, and DISC instruments evaluated strictly according to psychological manuals.
2. **De-escalated Brand Identity:** Moving away from a rigid military command system toward a modern, welcoming mental fitness sanctum.
3. **Actionable Personal Insight:** Detailed analysis of strengths, growth opportunities, and tailored self-regulation tools.
4. **Holistic Accompaniment:** Longitudinal habit tracking (sleep, hydration, movement, breathing, journaling) and ecological mood monitoring.
5. **Absolute Privacy & Trust:** Transparent data ownership, encrypted sessions, and confidential clinical standards.
